💡 About this tool

The Image Watermark Remover helps you remove watermarks, logos, timestamps, and other unwanted objects from your images — all directly in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Three removal modes: Smart Fill auto-blends the selection using surrounding pixels (best for simple backgrounds), Clone Stamp lets you sample a clean area and paint over the watermark (best for complex backgrounds), and Manual Brush paints a solid color (best for borders and edges).

Built on the HTML5 Canvas API, everything runs client-side. Your images stay on your device — private, secure, and fast.

📋 How to use

  1. Upload your image. Drag and drop an image onto the upload zone, or click to browse. The image loads instantly in the preview.
  2. Choose a tool mode. Smart Fill for auto-blending flat backgrounds, Clone Stamp for textured or complex areas, Brush for painting solid colors.
  3. For Smart Fill: Click and drag to draw a rectangle over the watermark. Release to auto-fill. Use the color picker (💉) to sample the exact background color first.
  4. For Clone Stamp: Click on a clean area to set the source point, then paint over the watermark. The source follows your brush movement.
  5. Zoom in for precision work using the slider below the image. Use Undo if you make a mistake.
  6. Download your cleaned image in PNG format. Or copy to clipboard to paste elsewhere.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The entire tool runs in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your image is loaded into memory locally and never sent to any server. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool still works.

How does Smart Fill work?

Smart Fill analyzes the pixels along the border of your selection and interpolates them across the selected area. For each pixel inside the selection, it computes a weighted blend of the nearest border pixels on each side. This works best on smooth or gradient backgrounds.

Can I remove watermarks from any image?

The tool works best when the watermark sits on a relatively uniform or smooth background. For complex textured backgrounds, use Clone Stamp mode. For very intricate backgrounds, manual editing with professional software may yield better results.

What file formats are supported?

You can load JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP images. The output is always saved as PNG to preserve quality (no compression artifacts). You can convert the downloaded PNG to other formats using our Image Converter.

Does this work on mobile devices?

Yes, the tool works on mobile browsers. However, precise watermark removal is much easier on desktop with a mouse. On touch devices, use pinch-to-zoom and the zoom slider for better precision.

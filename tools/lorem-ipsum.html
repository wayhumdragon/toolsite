💡 About this tool

The Lorem Ipsum Generator produces dummy placeholder text for use in design mockups, website wireframes, and layout testing. Lorem Ipsum has been the printing and typesetting industry's standard dummy text since the 1500s. It's derived from a scrambled passage of Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum (45 BC), making it look like natural language without being readable — so viewers focus on the design, not the content.

This tool offers three generation modes: paragraphs (for testing long-form content layouts), sentences (for heading/subtitle placeholders), and words (for button labels, navigation items, or taglines). All text is generated locally in your browser — nothing is stored or transmitted.

📋 How to use

  1. Choose a generation mode. Select Paragraphs (3-5 for page layouts), Sentences (for short text blocks), or Words (for labels and tags).
  2. Set the quantity. Enter a number or use the preset buttons — 1, 3, 5, or 10 of the selected unit.
  3. Generate and copy. Click Generate to fill the output area with Lorem Ipsum text. Click "Copy All" to copy it to your clipboard for pasting into your design tool.

❓ Frequently asked questions

What is Lorem Ipsum and why use it?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy placeholder text used in design since the 1500s. It mimics the visual rhythm of real text without being readable — letting clients focus on layout and typography rather than content.

How much text should I generate?

For headings, 1-2 sentences is enough. For full page layouts, 3-5 paragraphs is typical. For multi-page mockups, 5-10 paragraphs provides enough content to test line wrapping and column flow.

Is the generated text stored or shared?

No. All text generation happens locally in your browser. Nothing is stored, logged, or transmitted to any server.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes. The Lorem Ipsum Generator works on all modern browsers including iOS Safari and Android Chrome.

💡 About this tool

The PDF Whiteout tool lets you cover, hide and redact content in PDF documents directly in your browser. Think of it as digital correction fluid — draw a rectangle over any text, image, or area you want to conceal. The rectangle is permanently embedded into the PDF when you download it.

Key features: color picker to match the page background for invisible covers, custom color rectangles for highlighting or annotation, text overlay to add new text on top of covered areas, and multi-page support (navigate between pages, covers stay on their original page).

All processing uses pdf-lib and pdf.js — both run entirely in your browser. Your document never leaves your device. No upload, no server, no privacy risk.

Common use cases: redacting sensitive info before sharing, covering watermarks, whiteout for form corrections, hiding personal data in screenshots of PDFs, and annotating documents with colored highlights.

📋 How to use

  1. Load your PDF. Drag and drop a PDF file onto the upload zone, or click to browse. The first page renders instantly.
  2. Pick a cover color. Choose from the preset colors (white, black, gray) or use the eyedropper tool to sample a color directly from the page. This is the key to invisible covers — pick the exact background color.
  3. Draw cover rectangles. Click and drag on the preview to draw a rectangle. Each cover appears in the list. Navigate pages with the Prev/Next buttons. Covers are per-page.
  4. Optionally add text. Expand "Add Text Over Cover" and type text to embed on top of a covered area. Choose font size and color.
  5. Download. Click Download PDF to save the modified document with all covers and text permanently embedded.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The entire tool runs in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDF is loaded into memory locally and never sent to any server. pdf.js renders the preview, and pdf-lib modifies the document — both are client-side libraries. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool still works.

How does the color picker work?

Click the eyedropper icon, then click any point on the PDF preview. The tool samples the exact pixel color at that point and sets it as your current cover color. This lets you match the page background perfectly for invisible covers. The last 5 picked colors are remembered.

Can the cover rectangles be removed later?

Once you download the modified PDF, the rectangles are permanently embedded into the document — they become part of the PDF content and cannot be removed with this tool. However, while editing, you can delete any cover before downloading.

Does this work on mobile devices?

Basic functionality works on mobile (upload, preview, draw covers, download). However, the drawing experience is best on desktop with a mouse. On touch devices, tap-and-drag works but may be less precise.
